Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Pull back on your half of the screen to aim, then release to shoot. Hits push the monster toward your rival; a hit on the glowing weak point pushes it much further. Arrows that hit the spikes bounce off. HOW TO PLAY in the menu has the details.

How do I play with a friend?

On one device: choose 2 PLAYERS · ONE DEVICE. Online: tap PLAY ONLINE → CREATE ROOM and tap SHARE INVITE. Your friend taps the link, or enters the room code under PLAY ONLINE and taps JOIN.

The invite link didn't open the game.

Make sure ARC is installed, then tap the link again. You can always open the game and enter the room code by hand.

I got disconnected during a match.

The game pauses and tries to reconnect automatically for about 20 seconds. If you or your rival don't come back in time, the player who stayed wins the match.

The game is crashing or running slow.

Please make sure your device's OS is up to date. If the issue persists, email us with your device model and OS version.
